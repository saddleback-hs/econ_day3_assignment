Q2 — Technology Improves
Click "Q2: Show Tech Shift". A new technology makes robots twice as fast to produce. Describe what changed on the graph and why only one end of the curve moved.
Haz clic en "Q2: Show Tech Shift". Una nueva tecnología hace los robots el doble de rápido. Describe qué cambió y por qué solo un extremo de la curva se movió.
Q3 — A Drought Hits
Click "Q3: Show Drought Shift". A drought wipes out 20% of farm workers. Describe what changed and why only one end of the curve moved this time.
Haz clic en "Q3: Show Drought Shift". Una sequía elimina el 20% de los trabajadores agrícolas. Describe qué cambió y por qué solo un extremo de la curva se movió.
Q4 — Your World, Your PPC
Think about something in your own life, your school, or your neighborhood where resources (time, people, space, equipment) are NOT being used to their full potential.
